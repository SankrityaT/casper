# Reason, Praxos, Brycent's post, a16z (Oct 30)

## Brycent's post (@brycent, Oct 29; 36 likes, 23 replies)
- **Post:** "Whose making open router for everyone's AI agents to talk to each other in one chat 😭"
- **Replies:** mostly founders promoting their own versions: Praxos, Reason, Jinxo, Plasma Radio, Swarm, TalentOS and NebulaAI.
- **Useful lines:**
  - "the shared thing probably shouldn't be the conversation it should be the work" (@TheRealSergio91)
  - "Routing messages between agents is the easy layer. The hard part is identity and permissions: which agent may act on whose behalf, and who's accountable" (@seridarivus13)
  - Doubters: "not sure the utility in having my agent talk to some one else's agent?"

## Reason Machines (reasonmachines.com, YC Spring 2026)
- **Pitch:** "All your agents. One team. We built a frontier coding agent. Now it works with all your others."
- **Reason Network** (Oct 16): an open-source peer-to-peer network where your agents and your teammates' agents talk to each other. It covers Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Gemini CLI.
- **App:**
  - A sessions list, then a thread with named sub-agents shown as coloured dots ("Miso reproduced it", "Juniper traced…", "Pixel edited 2 files").
  - A right pane with **Review / Desktop / Terminal** tabs. Desktop shows the agent's computer with a cursor labelled "Reason" and a **Take control** button.
  - The PR is merged from inside the app, with a playtest recording. It also has a Slack app.
- **Look:**
  - near-white `#fcfcfc`, the system font, 1-px greys
  - tiny type
  - black pill buttons
  - one soft photographic backdrop behind the app
  - no colour except the agent dots

## Praxos (praxos.ai)
- **Pitch:** "Message your team and AI agents." Hand work from the team to Claude or Codex without re-explaining; it remembers what people and AI said and did.
- **Look:** warm off-white, Instrument Sans, centred text, thin geometric line art.
