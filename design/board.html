01 The cast

One material, eight silhouettes.

The same rules as Juno's agents. Every agent is the same matte pearl, lit the same way, with the same eyes. Only the silhouette and the aura change. Hover a card to wake one up, click it to make it happy.

02 Faces and states

The eyes carry the state.

One set of states for every agent, made from Juno's dot eyes plus Locked In's brows and a one-stroke mouth. In Listening, the eyes follow your cursor. Pick an agent and a state to see it.

03 A message in flight, over Agent Relay

You can watch them talk.

Every Relay message is drawn as the real note, flying from one agent to the other along an arc in the sender's colour. The sender squashes and watches it go. The receiver tracks it, perks up when it lands, and a ring in the sender's colour spreads. A faint trail stays, so the room shows who has been talking to whom.

Three tones of one hue. It's the agent's colour everywhere: its glow, its notes, its trail, its cursor on the canvas and its VM frame.
