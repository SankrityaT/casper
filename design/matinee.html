Every agent brings a prop.

The prop says what the agent does at a glance. Shadow bodies with glowing eyes, plus a few lit details: pack lights, goggle lenses, a headset mic, a candle flame. Everything stays a silhouette, so the moon and the eyes do the acting.

These are a nod to old ghost movies, not copies. No real logos or characters, and no slashed-ghost sign. That matters if Casper becomes a real company. The name "Casper" itself also needs a trademark check before the YC app.
