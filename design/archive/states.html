The canvas pieces the coding agent should copy as-is: agent cards, computer frames, ask cards, banners and toasts. Each tile names the state, what causes it, and which parts it uses. Colours carry meaning: orange means a person is needed, amber means something looks wrong, red means it failed, and blue means a person is driving.
